# Northwind identity

## Idea

Three independent rounded-square bytes increase in size and rise from observation to signal, expressing ranking movement without relying on the initial `A`. The unequal sizes prevent the mark from reading as an ellipsis, dice face, or app grid.

## Palette

- Signal Blue: `#0066CC`
- Night: `#17181B`
- Paper: `#F7F8FA`
- Mist: `#DCE7F3`
- Pale Blue: `#66B2FF`

## Assets

- `northwind-mark.svg`: primary three-byte mark; minimum 16 px.
- `northwind-icon.svg`: primary continuous-corner app icon; minimum 24 px.
- `northwind-lockup.svg`: primary horizontal lockup; minimum 120 px wide.
- `northwind-icon-180.png`: Apple touch icon.
- `northwind-icon-512.png`: Stripe and high-resolution raster icon.
- `northwind-logo-512x128.png`: Stripe horizontal logo.

Keep one mark geometry across every surface. Do not add gradients, shadows, sparkles, outlines, or connecting lines. Maintain clear space around the mark equal to the middle byte width.
